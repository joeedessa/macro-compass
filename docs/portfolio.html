Snapshot

Every open position, enriched hourly. Positions are equally weighted here — the export carried symbols and names but no share counts, so nothing on this page is a money figure. Read it as exposure by count and by event, not by value.

Big moves today

Ranked two ways, because they answer different questions. Against its own range divides today's move by the name's 14-day average true range — a 3% day in something that normally moves 0.8% is the tell, and a 6% day in a junior explorer is Tuesday. Largest absolute is the raw percentage. A name appearing in both is worth opening.

At the edges

Positions pressing a boundary. At means within 2% of the level, since an exact tick is rare and the useful question is whether it is testing it. The all-time high is as far back as the feed reaches, which for a recent listing is not very far — treat it as "highest ever recorded here" rather than a guarantee. RSI is the 14-day.

Structure

Where the book sits against its 50 and 200 on the daily, weekly and monthly, and which positions have just changed sides. Computed hourly rather than in the browser, so 405 names cost nothing to read. Closes only — a wick through a level that closed back above it counts as held.

Where the book actually sits

Exposure by count, with the average move of each bucket today — so you can see not just where you are concentrated but whether that concentration is working. Theme and sector count companies; exchange and currency count listings, because three names are held in both Toronto and New York and are genuinely two holdings there but one company here. Theme names link through to the rest of the dashboard.

Earnings

What was just delivered against what was expected, and what is coming. The reaction column is the move since the day of the report — the market's verdict rather than the headline number.

Dividends

Ex-dates you are about to trade through, and the payments that look stretched. A payout ratio above 100% means the dividend exceeds earnings; above 80% into falling earnings is the position a cut usually comes from. This flags arithmetic, not intent — no cut has been announced in any of these.

News on the names that moved

Headlines are pulled only for positions that did something — a move of two average ranges or more, a result inside three days, or a flagged dividend. Querying all 405 hourly would be noise; this keeps it to what changed.
