Cross-asset — what is driving what

Each pair puts an official macro series next to the market that trades off it, over the last three months, and says whether they are moving together or fighting. The relationship breaking is usually more informative than either leg on its own — gold rising into rising real yields, or equity rising while credit spreads widen, is the market telling you the obvious driver is not the one in charge.

US equity internals

What the index is hiding: which size and style box is actually leading, whether cyclicals are beating defensives, and how much of the market is participating. Uses the relative-strength window selected below.

Structure

Position against the 50 and 200 on the daily, weekly and monthly, across every instrument on this page — equities, rates, credit, FX and the commodity reference — plus anything that has just changed sides. Cross-asset is where the weekly and monthly averages earn their keep: long duration losing its monthly 200 is a regime statement, not a wobble.

Signal scan — moving-average crosses

Every cross in the last ten sessions across all instruments, laid out along the escalation ladder. Left to right: 5-EMA × 21 is a timing trigger only; price × 50 marks the swing-level break; 21-EMA × 50 the momentum turn; price × 200 is the regime call; 50 × 200 confirms it late but whipsaws least. Price leads its own averages, so names light up on the left first — over five years price crossed the 200 before the golden cross in every case, by a median of 18 sessions, but fewer than half of those price crosses ever reached a golden cross. Whipsawed crosses stay listed and flagged: a market that keeps crossing a level is telling you the level is contested.
