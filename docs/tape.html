Read of the session

Two reads, because at most hours of the day they disagree: the session that has finished, and the one already under way. The second is built only from instruments that are trading at this moment and re-reads itself every minute.

Market news

Headlines from Bloomberg's public markets and economics feeds, newest first. Titles and links only — the stories stay on Bloomberg.

Sessions

Which cash equity sessions are trading right now. Everything below is the current session where a market is open, and the last completed session where it is not — each card says which.

Around the world

Every region's own indices, quoted in their own currency and measured against their own exchange's previous close — so Asia reads as Asia saw it. Blocks run in session order, which is the order the day actually happens in.

Cross-asset

What is trading right now. Index futures run almost around the clock, so this is a real read on the US session hours before it opens — the paths are five-minute bars against each contract's own previous settlement. Every card carries the age of its last tick.

Movers

Ranked by size of move against each instrument's own 60-day volatility, so a 2% day in something that never moves outranks a 4% day in something that always does. The σ figure is that ratio.

Breadth

Share of each block trading higher on the session — where participation is broad and where it is a handful of names.

On the calendar

What is coming up on the watchlist, nearest first, from the TradingView events feed. Anything due today or tomorrow is called out; the rest is listed with its distance so the section still tells you something on the many days when nothing reports.
