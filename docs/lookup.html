Pick a subject and this pulls together everything the rest of the dashboard knows about it — the instruments that express it, the macro series that drive it and whether those relationships are currently behaving, the relative strength inside its own complex, any moving-average crosses, and the names on your watchlist that ride on it. Twelve of these are hand-written dossiers with a thesis and drivers; the rest are every country, theme and commodity the dashboard tracks, generated from the same lists those pages use. Whichever you pick is highlighted wherever it appears among its peers.
