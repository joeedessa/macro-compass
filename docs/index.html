Macro Compass — morning read

Regime

Seven risk conditions, each either met or not. Read the disagreements, not just the count — a high score with credit failing is a different market than one with vol failing.

Notable moves

Ranked by how unusual each move is against that instrument's own recent daily volatility, not by raw size — so a quiet market making a big move outranks a noisy one drifting. Anything at or above 2σ is worth a second look.

Relative strength

What is leading what. Each ratio states plainly what a rising line means, with the 200-day average drawn in grey so trend changes are visible rather than inferred.

Markets

Every instrument, with trend state against its 200-day average and position in the 52-week range. Yields move in basis points, everything else in percent. Colour marks the direction of a move, not whether it is good news — a rising yield or a stronger dollar is green here, and whether that helps depends on your book. Scroll sideways on narrow screens.

Macro fundamentals

Slow-moving official data — inflation, labour, growth and policy rates. These set the backdrop the market read sits inside.

Prices from Yahoo Finance, fetched live in your browser on each load (delayed, not real-time; no account or API key involved). Macro fundamentals from FRED, ECB and the World Bank, refreshed hourly — raw data: macro.json. Source: joeedessa/macro-compass. Not investment advice.
