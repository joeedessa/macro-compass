Regime

Seven risk conditions, each either met or not. Read the disagreements, not just the count — a high score with credit failing is a different market than one with vol failing. Press i on any card for how the signal is conventionally interpreted.

Notable moves

Ranked by how unusual each move is against that instrument's own recent daily volatility, not by raw size — so a quiet market making a big move outranks a noisy one drifting. Anything at or above 2σ is worth a second look.

Economic Modern Family

Six sector ETFs standing for six parts of the real economy, a framework published by Mish Schneider of MarketGauge (plus Bitcoin, added later). The question is whether they move together: a split family — banks or transportation dropping away while semis carry the index — says the advance is narrowing. Independent implementation, not affiliated with MarketGauge.

Relative strength

What is leading what. Each ratio states plainly what a rising line means. Switch the window to separate a genuine trend change from noise — a rotation visible at 1M but absent at 6M is usually noise.

Markets

Yields move in basis points, everything else in percent. Colour marks the direction of a move, not whether it is good news — a rising yield or a stronger dollar is green here, and whether that helps depends on your book.

Macro fundamentals

Slow-moving official data — inflation, labour, growth and policy rates. These set the backdrop the market read sits inside.
