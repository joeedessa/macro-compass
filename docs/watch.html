Add a setup

Four levels per trade, all optional except the alert: alert pings when price is beyond your level; negation is the invalidation — a close beyond it archives the trade automatically; target is where the thesis pays; breakthrough confirms entry when a close clears it (above for longs, below for shorts). The shared list loads from the repo on every device; your edits (negations, manual moves, extra setups) stay in this browser and re-apply on top. States are recomputed from price history on every load, so every device shows the same breakouts without any syncing.
