Trade log

An independent journal of actual entries and exits. Logging here never moves a setup between the tables above and the tables never write here — the Log button on any row only pre-fills this form. Price defaults to the settled close of the trade date and follows the date until you type a price yourself; after that the date leaves it alone. The shared log lives in the repo (data/tradelog.json); fills you add here are kept in this browser and included in Export, so committing the exported file publishes them.

Cross scan

The same moving-average ladder used on Markets, Themes and Countries, run over the setups on this list. Left to right is the escalation order — each step costs time and buys confidence, and price leads its own averages arithmetically, so a name normally lights up on the left and works rightward. Switch the timeframe to read the same ladder at a horizon where a crossing is rare enough to mean something: a 200 on weekly bars is close to four years, on monthly bars about seventeen.

Earnings & news detail

Jump here from the ▮ icon on any row. What the last quarter delivered against expectations — including how the market traded it — plus the consensus for the coming report and the street's price target, plotted against your own. Where a name has reported recently, the wire block classifies the language of post-release headlines — guidance raised or cut, beat, miss — and links each headline verbatim. Numeric estimates are analyst consensus from TradingView rather than company-issued figures, and everything stays in the instrument's listing currency.

Prices from Yahoo Finance, fetched live on each load (delayed, not real-time). Each instrument is quoted and evaluated in its own listing currency — nothing is converted. Rules are evaluated against daily closes only — intraday touches do not count, by design, so signals match end-of-day discipline. A long confirms on a close above its trigger, a short on a close below it. The shared setups live in the public repo (data/watchlist.json); personal edits and additions stay in this browser's localStorage. Part of Macro Compass. Not investment advice.
