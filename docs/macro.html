Sentiment & positioning

Positioning data rather than surveys: what hedgers and small speculators actually hold (CFTC, weekly), and what the volatility curve is pricing. Sentiment signals are contrarian at extremes and noise in the middle — the percentile matters, not the sign.

Official flows

Who is actually financing the US deficit, and what the largest reserve managers hold. Auction bid-to-cover and the indirect bidder share are the closest public read on foreign official demand — no country publishes the currency breakdown of its own reserves.

Macro fundamentals

Slow-moving official data — inflation, labour, growth and policy rates. These set the backdrop the market read sits inside.

Official data from FRED, the CFTC, TreasuryDirect, the ECB and the World Bank, refreshed hourly — raw data: macro.json. Part of Macro Compass. Not investment advice.
