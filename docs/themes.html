Signal scan — moving-average crosses

Theme ETFs that crossed a key average in the last ten sessions. Columns run left to right along the escalation ladder — 5-EMA × 21 is a timing trigger, price × 50 the swing break, 21-EMA × 50 the momentum turn, price × 200 the regime call and 50 × 200 the late confirmation that whipsaws least. Earlier signals fire first and fail often; later ones arrive weeks after and rarely lie. Whipsaws are flagged, not hidden.
